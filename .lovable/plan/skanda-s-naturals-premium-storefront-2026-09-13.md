# Skanda’s Naturals premium storefront

## Direction
- Build a polished one-page storefront at `/` using the supplied references as inspiration, not as embedded page images.
- Use the uploaded transparent Skanda’s Naturals logo in the navigation and footer.
- Create a warm, minimal visual system: soft off-white space, deep botanical green, near-black typography, and restrained fresh-lime accents.
- Use a premium geometric grotesk pairing comparable to the “Bring Fresh Growth to Agriculture” reference, with large editorial headings and clean body copy.
- Keep sections visually connected with overlapping image edges, soft fades, and shared backgrounds rather than isolated floating cards.

## Page structure
1. **Navigation** — logo, compact section links, and a WhatsApp “Order now” action.
2. **Video introduction** — full-bleed supplied video with readable text in its clear region, subtle contrast treatment, one WhatsApp action, and mobile-safe cropping.
3. **Brand story** — concise positioning around traditional wood cold pressing, purity, and everyday cooking.
4. **Oil origins** — use the three supplied root cross-section images as large backgrounds for coconut, sesame, and groundnut stories, placing content only in clear image regions.
5. **Product collection** — Coconut Oil, Groundnut Oil, and Sesame Oil, each offering 250 ml, 500 ml, and 1 L selectors.
6. **Process and values** — a restrained, image-led explanation of wood cold pressing without unsupported health claims.
7. **WhatsApp ordering panel** — clear final call to order or ask a question.
8. **Full footer** — logo, product links, page links, WhatsApp contact, copyright, and a concise brand statement.

## Ordering behavior
- Every product/size combination will open WhatsApp to **+91 63743 92488**.
- The message will be prefilled with the exact oil name and selected size, for example: “Hi Skanda’s Naturals, I’d like to order Groundnut Oil — 500 ml.”
- Product choices will use accessible selectors and clear order buttons; no prices will be invented because none were supplied.

## Responsive and interaction details
- Design mobile-first, then tune tablet and desktop layouts separately.
- Keep navigation, type, video crop, product controls, and footer usable without overlap at narrow widths.
- Use restrained transitions between sections and gentle reveal motion, with reduced-motion support.
- Add video fallback/poster handling, lazy-load supporting imagery, descriptive image text, keyboard focus states, and strong color contrast.
